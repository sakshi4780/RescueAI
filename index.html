What pushed the score

Bars right of centre raise abandonment risk; left lowers it (log-odds contribution).

Assumptions

Model: targeted fixes (delivery date, cheaper alternative) recover ~16% of abandoners with no discount. A discount adds recoveries with diminishing returns, but is also given to people who would have bought anyway (assumed 50% of abandoner count). These are assumptions to tune, not measured results.

Impact

Interview cheat-sheet

  • Problem: carts get abandoned; blanket discounts burn margin. RescueAI estimates the most likely driver and picks the cheapest effective intervention.
  • Data: simulated behavioural sessions (price, time on product, shipping-page visits, price-comparison clicks, cart additions, checkout dwell, delivery promise). Real customer-level checkout data is not public.
  • Model: logistic scorer. The weights here are hand-set to mimic what a model trained on the simulated data would learn; swap in Random Forest/XGBoost trained with scikit-learn for production.
  • Reason engine: compares delivery, price and friction signals and reports the largest share. It estimates a likely driver, not a certain one.
  • Evaluation to quote: AUC / precision-recall for the predictor, and an A/B test on checkout conversion and net revenue per session for the intervention.
  • North-star metric: net incremental revenue = recovered revenue minus discount cost minus leakage.
  • PM point: a prediction is only useful if it changes what the product does. Intervention choice is the product.
